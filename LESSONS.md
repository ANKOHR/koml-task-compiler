# Lessons for KOML

Explicit requirement clauses are a workable boundary for a deterministic task compiler. The synthetic tests validate preservation and structural review against planted ground truth. They do not validate understanding of arbitrary natural language, semantic acceptance quality, or usefulness to Henry.

The broad assumption that deterministic parsing alone can formalise arbitrary objectives is unsupported. This implementation exposes missing requirements and unrecognized clauses rather than inventing them. Real-use accuracy and adoption remain unmeasured.

Operating rule: preserve every source requirement, make unresolved text visible, and distinguish structural completeness from semantic approval. Keep independent expected results outside the production computation path and mutate inputs to prove counts are computed.
